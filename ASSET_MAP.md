# Arkvoid Image Asset Map

Every visual asset for the Arkvoid website is defined below.

## Production Guidelines
- **Export Format**: Optimised JPG or WebP.
- **Budget**: Maximum **350 KB** per image.
- **Framing Constraint**: `device-today.jpg` and `device-world.jpg` **MUST** share the identical camera angle, lens focal length, and room framing to achieve a seamless comparative dissolve.
- **Atmosphere Fallback**: When photos are not present, `SceneImage` automatically displays the calibrated Atmosphere Plate (cadet-deep canvas, distant ember lantern radial at 10%, gas haze, 18% opacity centered logo arch, and scene chip).
